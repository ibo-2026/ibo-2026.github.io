About

Where building operations gets intelligent

Intelligent Building Operations (IBO) covers advanced design and control methods, data analytics, fault detection and diagnostics, predictive maintenance, and other software and instrumentation techniques applied to heating, cooling, ventilation, lighting, and other building systems.

IBO technologists generally aim to improve the occupant experience, reduce operating and maintenance costs, mitigate pollutant emissions, and enhance interactivity between buildings and power grids.

The 2026 workshop runs as one day with two sessions: a morning on research, with speakers from industry, academia, and national laboratories discussing recent findings and newly developed tools; and an afternoon on education, examining how universities teach IBO today and how curricula could better serve the evolving needs of research groups and industry practitioners.
